Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

NEXORA respects the privacy of its website visitors. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you visit our website, how it may be used and what choices may be available to you.

About this policy: This policy describes the current informational website. If advertising, analytics, contact forms or other services are introduced, this policy will be reviewed and updated as necessary.

1. Information We May Receive

NEXORA is an informational website. Visitors can browse its articles and pages without submitting personal information.

If you contact us by email, we may receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, name and the contents of your message.

Website hosting infrastructure may also process technical information associated with website requests, such as IP addresses, browser details and access logs, according to the hosting provider's practices.

2. How Information May Be Used

Information received through email may be used to:

  • Respond to questions and enquiries.
  • Review suggestions and reader feedback.
  • Investigate reported content errors.
  • Maintain correspondence when necessary.

Technical information processed by hosting infrastructure may be used by the provider for website operation, security and troubleshooting.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small files that websites or third-party services may store on a visitor's device. They can support functions such as remembering preferences, measuring activity or delivering advertising.

The current NEXORA website pages are designed as static informational pages and do not intentionally set their own tracking cookies. However, hosting infrastructure or third-party services may operate independently.

If additional services that use cookies are introduced, this policy and any required consent arrangements will be updated.

4. Advertising and Google AdSense

NEXORA does not currently display Google AdSense advertisements through the website.

If advertising services are introduced in the future, advertising providers may use cookies, device information or similar technologies to deliver, measure and personalise advertisements, depending on the service and applicable settings.

Before advertising is enabled, NEXORA will review the relevant provider requirements, privacy disclosures and applicable consent requirements.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, visit: Google Privacy Policy .

5. Third-Party Services and External Links

NEXORA may link to external websites and resources for additional information. When you visit an external website, its own privacy policy and terms apply.

We do not control the privacy practices of third-party websites. Visitors should review their policies before sharing personal information.

6. Data Security

We aim to handle information responsibly and take reasonable steps appropriate to the website. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Data Retention

Emails and related correspondence may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, maintain relevant records or address legitimate website administration needs.

Hosting providers may retain technical logs according to their own retention practices.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information. These may include requesting access, correction or deletion of information, or objecting to certain processing.

To make a privacy-related enquiry, contact us using the email address below.

9. Children's Privacy

NEXORA is a general informational website and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

Please do not send sensitive personal information through email or other communication channels.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website, its services or applicable requirements change. Any revised version will be published on this page with an updated date.
